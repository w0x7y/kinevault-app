# KineVault Track motion ad

Concept: Small daily actions become visible progress, with Kine there at the finish.
Hero: A blue orb becomes a nutrition ring, a weight plate, and a graph point.
Callback: The orb closes into a blue brand seal beside the original Kine mascot.
Memorable moment: A weight plate flattens into a graph point and draws a rising line.
Palette: midnight #121E30, electric blue #367CF6, ice #EEF4FB, sky #79B7E8, amber #E5B365, lilac #B69ADC.
Type: Bricolage Grotesque 800 for editorial headlines; Comfortaa 600/700 for product copy and brand.
Format: 1920 × 1080, 60 fps, 11 seconds, synthesized score and continuous ElevenLabs female narration.
Vertical export: 1080 × 1920, 9:16, with the same 11-second timing and narration. Headlines sit above centered phones; the graph spans the lower frame; the closing mascot sits below the brand name. The two top labels are removed in both formats. Settled portrait content stays between approximately y=200 and y=1665, with extra room on the right for social controls.
Treatment: Vector app panels are illustrative compositions based on implemented features, with sample data.
Anti-default: Characterful type, a product-specific through-line, object-led transitions, one physical motion per beat, no stock footage or gradient backgrounds.
